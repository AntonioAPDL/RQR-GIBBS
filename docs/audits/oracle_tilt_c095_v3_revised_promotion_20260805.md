# Oracle-tilt version-3 revised illustration promotion

## Decision

The complete version-3 single-data illustration is promoted to the manuscript.
Five family--target cells pass every original computational and recovery gate.
The dynamic shortest-window (SH) cell is accepted under a disclosed revised
width-contrast relative-error tolerance of 0.21. Its observed error is
0.202622544829516, so it did not pass the original 0.20 threshold and is not
described as a prespecified strict pass.

This decision concerns a descriptive, single-data illustration of interval-root
generalized-posterior behavior. It is not a repeated-sample calibration claim,
a response-likelihood analysis, or posterior-predictive response evidence.

## Why the revised tolerance is acceptable here

The 0.20 boundary was a conservative illustration-recovery screen rather than
a mathematical validity boundary. The miss is 0.002623 in absolute relative
error, or about 1.31% of the original threshold. Treating that isolated miss as
an automatic rejection would discard an otherwise informative richer example
without changing the scientific interpretation of the fitted interval roots.
The threshold change is therefore recorded explicitly rather than hidden or
retroactively labeled prospective.

Computational integrity remains non-negotiable. The accepted dynamic SH result:

- completed five chains with 12,000 retained draws per chain;
- reproduced all 15 original 6,000-draw prefixes bitwise;
- passed all 137 maintained MCMC diagnostics;
- required zero numerical repairs;
- retained exact source/runtime binding; and
- passed the monitored resource and process-group contract.

The remaining discrepancy is a bounded recovery limitation for the frozen
illustration, not evidence of a failed sampler or an altered generalized-Bayes
target.

## Current rendering and execution policy

Version 3 is now the active manuscript rendering source. Version 2 remains a
validated previous bundle. Current `main` permits only lightweight audit,
rendering, and testing of the closed version-3 campaign. It does not authorize
another benchmark, acceptance run, execution, adjudication, seed search, or
same-data retuning.

For future illustration campaigns, computational-integrity requirements remain
hard. A single near-boundary recovery statistic should trigger a documented
scientific review rather than automatic rejection. Any threshold change must
remain visible in both machine-readable evidence and manuscript prose.
